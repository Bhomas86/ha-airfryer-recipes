# 0.1.0b20 – Selectable artwork styles A and B

[Deutsch](0.1.0b20.de.md) | [English](0.1.0b20.md)

The card editor now offers **Zone selection on the image** with both owner-approved designs:

- **A – Subtle outline:** the selected photo zone has a thin outline without a filled overlay. Small numbered markers identify the zones. The existing radio selection beside the image remains available.
- **B – Invisible touch areas:** the photo has no visible zone rectangles, filled overlays or numbers. The configured areas remain real tappable buttons, including on the selected zone. The side buttons show the selected zone with a border/background and retain its reported status. Keyboard-only focus remains visible for accessibility; this is not a persistent touch overlay.

The choice is saved per card as `zone_style: outline` or `zone_style: invisible`; A is the default when omitted. Images and relative x/y/width/height stay stored per device and are shared with its permitted users. Both styles use exactly the same saved areas. The original neutral illustration remains the fallback when no personalized artwork is available. No data migration, new device permission or appliance action is added. Selecting a zone still only selects it; sending remains a separate explicit action.

For B, open **Customize Airfryer** to set the photo and zone sizes/positions. Its editing rectangles stay visible so they can be dragged and adjusted numerically. Press **Save appearance** to commit the device artwork. The active dashboard then displays invisible areas at those positions. Choose A/B in the main card editor and use the HA footer **Save** to commit that card option. Changing the style does not overwrite the image, its coordinates, cooking drafts or last-sent history. The b19 refresh after appearance saving remains included; native b19 acceptance has not yet been reported.

Install v0.1.0b20 through HACS, restart HA and fully reopen the app once. Keep test mode enabled. In the card editor select B, save the card option, then set both zones over the photo in Customize Airfryer and save the appearance. On the active dashboard check that rectangles/numbers are absent; tap each configured image area and verify that the correct side button becomes selected. Repeat using the side buttons. Return to A and check a thin active outline and small markers. Try another graphic size and reopen the editor to verify that the style and device coordinates persist. No device transmission is required. Native HA/Companion visual and touch acceptance remains pending.
